# Life Journal — The Sims 4

Life Journal is a storytelling and life-tracking gameplay mod being developed for The Sims 4.

## Core Idea

> You create the Sim. Life Journal helps you tell, play, and remember their story.

Life Journal is designed to give players two ways to tell stories with their Sims:

### Free Journal
Players can create their own stories while Life Journal records important goals, relationships, experiences, and life events.

### Story Blueprints
Players can choose or generate structured stories containing dynamic objectives, decisions, complications, milestones, and consequences.

Story Blueprints provide direction without completely scripting a Sim's life.

Two Sims playing the same Blueprint should be able to experience very different stories.

## First Story Blueprint

### Finding My Lane

A personal-growth story about a Sim discovering who they are, what they enjoy, who belongs in their life, and what direction they want their future to take.

The first planned chapters are:

- Chapter I — Branching Out
- Chapter II — Choosing My Lane
- Chapter III — Outside Noise

### Confirmed Chapter Transition Rule

A chapter ends when **all of its chapter objectives are completed or its chapter timer expires**, whichever happens first. When another chapter exists, it begins from that outcome.

Completed and unfinished objective outcomes inform the next chapter's story. Chapter advancement does not require every objective to be completed when the chapter timer expires.

Still to define:
- The exact duration of each chapter in Sim days.
- Which objectives belong to the chapter completion set, including how optional objectives affect early completion.
- How unfinished objectives are resolved or carried forward.
- Pause behavior and how individual objective timers relate to the chapter timer.

## Project Status

Life Journal is currently in the **design and documentation stage**.

The project is being redesigned specifically for The Sims 4 after originally being conceptualized for The Sims 2.

Gameplay systems will be documented and researched before full implementation begins.

## Development Approach

Features should progress through:

**Concept → Design → Technical Research → Prototype → Testing → Implementation**

Design documentation should not be interpreted as an instruction to implement every planned feature immediately.S
